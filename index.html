Scholar Profile
Historian & Scholar

Prof. R. Mahalakshmi

Historian first, and Professor of Ancient & Early Medieval South Asia.

Recognized globally as one of the most important academics in the field of ancient and early medieval Indian and South Asian history. Her scholarly work spans religious iconography, textual hermeneutics, premodern state formation, and gender in early historical traditions.

Major Publications

Books & Monographs

Pioneering contributions to ancient South Asian religious history, iconographic transformations, and comparative historical studies.

2011 Monograph

The Making of the Goddess: Korravai-Durgā in the Tamil Traditions

A detailed study tracing the transformation of the indigenous war goddess Korravai into the Puranic goddess Durga in early South Indian traditions.

2015 Co-Author

Colonisation: A Comparative Study of India and Korea

Co-authored with Vyjayanti Raghavan. A comparative historical study examining colonial experiences and institutional trajectories across both regions.

2019 Editor

Art and History: Texts, Contexts and Visual Representations

A collection of scholarly essays analyzing the intersections between premodern artistic styles, textual prescriptions, and historical contexts in early medieval India.

2025 Co-Editor

Goddesses in South Asia: Traditions and Transformations

Co-edited with Malavika Binny and Megha Yadav. A multidisciplinary volume covering the diversity, development, and social roles of goddess traditions across South Asia.
