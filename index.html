Scholarship & Inquiry

Areas of Specialization

Deep thematic exploration into political economy, religious art history, gender discourse, and trans-regional exchanges in pre-modern South Asia.

Early Medieval State Structures

Analysis of political formation, Chola state legitimacy, revenue systems, and socio-economic dynamics across Tamilakam and South India.

Gender & Patriarchy in Early India

Critically interrogating women's roles, family structures, female ascetic traditions (such as Karaikkal Ammaiyar), and goddess worship in Cankam literature and Tamil Bhakti.

Brahmanical & Buddhist Sacred Art

Development of structural shrines, iconographic lineages, and temple architecture in South Asia and Southeast Asia.

Tamil & Sri Lankan Cultural Contacts

Historical linkages between Tamilakam and Sri Lanka (c. 300 BCE – 1300 CE), backed by field research supported by the Indo-Sri Lanka Foundation.

Brahmanical / Tribal Interactions

Tribal histories in pre-modern South Asia, highlighting assimilation, conflict, and religious syncretism between indigenous communities and institutionalized traditions.

Historiography & Epistemology

Methodological appraisals of ancient text traditions, Cankam literary prisms, oral histories, and colonial comparative frameworks (including Korea-India comparative studies).
