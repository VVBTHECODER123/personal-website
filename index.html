Biography

R. Mahalakshmi is Professor at the Centre for Historical Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. Her primary areas of research interest include early historical and early medieval South Asia, religion, state formation, iconography, and gender history.

She is the author of seminal works such as The Making of the Goddess: Korravai-Durga in the Tamil Traditions (Penguin, 2011) and The Book of Lakshmi (Penguin, 2009). Her extensive scholarship explores the intersection of religion, royal patronage, epigraphy, and material culture in South India.

Professor Mahalakshmi has served as Sectional President (Ancient India) for the Indian History Congress and active executive officer in prominent academic bodies, actively contributing to public discourse on history, secularism, and democratic heritage.
