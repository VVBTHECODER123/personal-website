Biography

R. Mahalakshmi is Professor at the Centre for Historical Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. Her research interests encompass the development of state institutions, as well as religious and artistic traditions across ancient and early medieval India, Nepal, and Sri Lanka.

Her monograph The Making of the Goddess: Korravai-Durgā in the Tamil Traditions (Penguin, 2011) was awarded the Professor Hiralal Gupta Prize for the Best Book by a Woman Historian by the Indian History Congress. She is also the author of The Book of Lakshmi (Penguin, 2009) and editor of Art and History: Texts, Contexts and Visual Representations in Ancient and Early Medieval India (Bloomsbury, 2020).

Elected President of the Ancient India Section of the Indian History Congress (2024), her ongoing projects examine performative traditions in early Tamil Nadu and the history of the Cholas.
